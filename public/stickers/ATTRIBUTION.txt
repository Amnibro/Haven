The built-in sticker artwork in this directory is original work drawn for
Haven. Every sticker is generated from tools/stickers/art.js with the
shapes in tools/stickers/kit.js: no third-party images, icon sets or emoji
fonts are used, and the lettering is hand-drawn strokes rather than a font.
Hex, the hexagon character, is based on Haven's own logo.

see LICENSE at the top of the repository).
